Eleanor stood at the window watching the rain hammer against the glass. She knew he would come. He always came when the weather was at its worst.

"You shouldn't be here," she said quietly, without turning around.

"I know that," James replied from the doorway. His voice was rough, but there was something else in it now — something she hadn't heard before. "But I couldn't stay away."

Eleanor closed her eyes for a moment. She felt the familiar mixture of relief and dread that always surfaced when he stood this close. "What do you want me to say?" she asked, her voice nearly breaking.

"Say yes," he whispered. "Just once. Say yes."
